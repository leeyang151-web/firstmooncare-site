Welcome to First Moon

We believe the weeks after birth should be for rest and recovery, with the right help close at hand.

Our approach

Inspired by a tradition of rest

Across Taiwan, Hong Kong and much of East Asia, the weeks after a birth are set aside for zuò yuèzi, 坐月子: time to rest, eat well and let the body recover, with family close by.

First Moon brings that idea to modern life in the UK. It is for every family who wants calm, steady help after a birth, whether or not the tradition is their own.

Rest

Real sleep for the mother, and time for her body to recover.

Nourishment

Warm, nourishing postnatal food, made with care.

Support

An experienced pair of hands for the baby and the household.

The name

初月, the first moon

In Chinese, 月 is both moon and month. The first moon is the first month: the time a family keeps for rest, warmth and recovery after a birth.
