Our approach我們的理念

Inspired by a tradition of rest靈感來自傳統的產後休養

In many East Asian families, a new mother is looked after in the weeks after birth. She rests, eats warming food, and others help with the baby. In Mandarin this is called zuò yuèzi.在許多東亞家庭裡，產後的媽媽會被細心照顧：好好休息、吃溫補的餐點，並由家人幫忙照顧寶寶，這就是坐月子。

We take the heart of that idea and shape it for modern families in the UK, whatever their background, for as long as they need it.我們延續這份用心，為在英國的現代家庭量身打造。不論背景，都能依您的需要安排。

Rest休息

Fewer chores and fewer visitors, so your body can recover.減少家務與訪客，讓身體好好恢復。

Nourishment營養

Warm, balanced meals and soups, cooked for you.溫暖均衡的餐點與湯品，為您用心烹調。

Support支援

An experienced pair of hands with the baby, so you can sleep.有經驗的人幫忙照顧寶寶，讓您可以安心入睡。
