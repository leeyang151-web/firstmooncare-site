Calmer early weeks for new families in the UK.

給英國的新手家庭，安穩的產後時光。

In many East Asian families, the weeks after birth are a time to rest, eat warming food and let others help with the baby. We want to make that kind of care easy to arrange in the UK, for as long as your family needs it.

在許多東亞家庭裡，產後是好好休息、吃溫補料理、讓別人幫忙照顧寶寶的時候。我們希望在英國也能輕鬆安排這樣的照顧，並依您家庭的需要安排時間長短。

The tradition傳統

Where the idea comes from靈感來自坐月子

Our inspiration is 坐月子 (zuò yuèzi) in Mandarin, or 坐月 (co5 jyut6) in Cantonese: the tradition of looking after a mother in the month after birth. It rests on a few simple ideas, and they are just as useful in the weeks and months that follow.

我們的靈感來自坐月子的傳統。它圍繞著幾個簡單的觀念，而這些觀念在產後的數週甚至數月同樣受用。

  • 息Rest休息

    Time to recover, with fewer chores and fewer visitors.

    減少家務與訪客，專心恢復體力。

  • 膳Nourishing food溫補飲食

    Warm soups and meals cooked for you, day after day.

    每天為您準備溫熱的湯品與餐點。

  • 助An extra pair of hands有人幫忙

    Someone experienced with newborns, so you can sleep.

    有經驗的人幫忙照顧新生兒，讓您可以好好睡覺。

What we're exploring我們正在規劃

How First Moon could help初月可以怎麼幫忙

These are ideas we are testing, not services you can book yet. Your answers decide which ones we build first.

以下是我們正在評估的構想，目前還不能預訂。您的意見將決定我們先做哪一項。

Postnatal carer at home到府產後照顧
An experienced carer who comes to your home to help with the baby, the household and your meals, for a few weeks or longer.有經驗的照顧者到府協助照顧寶寶、家務與飲食，可安排數週或更長時間。
Meal delivery產後餐外送
Traditional postnatal meals and soups, delivered to your door.傳統產後餐點與湯品，直接送到府上。
Newborn workshop新生兒照顧課程
A practical class for couples before the birth: feeding, bathing, sleep and planning your support after the birth.為準爸媽設計的實用課程：餵奶、洗澡、睡眠，以及產後支援規劃。
Later: a place to stay未來：產後住宿
Rooms where mother and baby are looked after during a stay, if there is enough demand.若需求足夠，我們希望提供讓媽媽與寶寶安心入住、受到照顧的空間。

First Moon offers rest, food and practical help. It is not a medical service. Your NHS maternity team and GP remain responsible for your health and your baby's health.初月提供的是休息、飲食與生活上的協助，並非醫療服務。您與寶寶的健康仍由 NHS 產科團隊及家庭醫師（GP）負責。

Privacy notice隱私權聲明

How we use your information我們如何使用您的資料

First Moon is a new project run by its founders in the UK. We are not yet a registered company; we will update this notice when FirstMoon Care Ltd is set up. Last updated 25 October 2026.

What we collect

  • Survey: your answers only. We do not ask for your name, email or contact details, so please don't add them in free-text answers.
  • Waitlist: your name, email address, due month (optional) and preferred language.

Why we use it

Survey answers help us decide whether and how to start First Moon. Waitlist details are used only to tell you when we open. Our legal basis is your consent. A due month is health information, so we ask for your explicit consent on the waitlist form.

Where it is kept and for how long

Both forms run on Google Forms, and the answers are stored in our Google account. Google may process data outside the UK under its standard safeguards. We keep survey answers until our research ends and then keep only totals. We delete waitlist details when you ask, or 12 months after you sign up if we have not launched.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your waitlist details, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing us. We never sell or share your details. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

初月是由英國創辦人經營的新計畫，目前尚未註冊公司；FirstMoon Care Ltd 成立後我們會更新本聲明。最後更新：2026 年 10 月 25 日。
