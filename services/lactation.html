Lactation consultants

Feeding help from a certified consultant

Breastfeeding, expressing and bottle-feeding questions answered at home by an IBCLC-certified lactation consultant, contracted by First Moon and working alongside your carer, in English or Mandarin.

What it will look like

One calm, qualified voice, in your own home

Feeding is where many new parents feel most alone, and where advice from everyone is loudest. This is the one part of First Moon we are already certain about: the consultants will be certified, contracted by us, and part of the care from the first week.

  • A home visit from an IBCLC-certified lactation consultant, usually in the first week home
  • Help with latch and positioning, supply questions, expressing and pumping, and combination or bottle feeding
  • A written plan that you and your carer follow between visits
  • Follow-up by phone or video, and a second visit when it is needed
  • Consultants who work in English and Mandarin, with Cantonese as the team grows
  • Working alongside your NHS maternity team and health visitor, never instead of them

How it might work

With a carer, on its own, or before the birth

The consultants are settled; the shapes around them are what we are testing. Price will be set with the families who answer the survey.

Within a carer package

A consultant visit built into The First Moon package, in the first week, with your carer in the room so the plan carries on after the visit.

On its own

A single home visit or a short series, booked through First Moon, for families who do not need a carer.

Before the birth

A session in the last weeks of pregnancy on what the first days of feeding really look like, for parents who like to prepare.

Who it is for

For anyone who wants feeding to feel less like a battle

  • Parents breastfeeding for the first time, or after a hard start
  • Parents combining breast and bottle, or bottle-feeding and wanting to do it well
  • Parents expressing or pumping, back at work or not
  • Families who want advice in Mandarin, from someone who understands the tradition's food and rest

Questions people ask

The honest answers, for now

Is this medical care?

A lactation consultant is a qualified professional, and feeding advice is exactly what an IBCLC is certified to give. First Moon itself is still not a medical service: your NHS maternity team and GP remain responsible for your health and your baby's health, and the consultant will send you to them for anything that needs a doctor.

Who are the consultants?

Certified lactation consultants holding the IBCLC, the international standard, contracted by First Moon rather than found for you. We will name each of them, with their certification, when the service opens.

Will the consultant and the carer talk to each other?

Yes, with your permission. The plan from the visit is shared with your carer, so the help carries on between visits instead of stopping at the door.

Can I see a consultant without having a carer?

That is one of the shapes we are testing. Tell us in the survey.

What would it cost?

Not set yet, and it will differ depending on whether the visit is part of a package. The survey asks what would feel fair.
